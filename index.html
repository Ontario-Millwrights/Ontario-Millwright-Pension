How the estimates work

Initial hours: 950, 1,500, 1,750, 1,870, then 2,000 per year. The fitter balances available annual and cumulative statement targets, limits hours to 0–5,000, and weakly favours the initial history. A close match does not establish the true annual hours.

Rates originate in the supplied pension calculator workbook, 1995–2025. The workbook's approximate 1.06 pension multiplier is included for 2024 and 2025. These are editable effective annual rates, not verified dated rate schedules. Midyear allocation, transfers and adjustments may prevent an exact match.

Estimated earnings = hours × JM wage × 1.10. This does not model apprentice rates, overtime premiums or other earnings. Hours are pension-credit hours and may differ from clock hours.

06 Earnings-based comparison

This earnings-based (service factor) comparison uses a similar earnings-and-service benefit structure to defined benefit plans such as the Ontario Teachers’ Pension Plan (OTPP) and OMERS: years of service × service factor × average earnings.

This is a simplified comparison, not an OTPP or OMERS pension estimate. It excludes plan-specific CPP integration, bridge benefits, indexing and retirement rules. OMERS uses best-five consecutive earnings; the selected earnings basis here may differ. OTPP formula · OMERS formula
